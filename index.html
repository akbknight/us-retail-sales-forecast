// Methodology & Assumptions

Data

FRED series RSXFS — Advance Retail and Food Services Sales, Seasonally Adjusted, monthly, millions of USD. 320 observations from Jan 2000 through Aug 2026. Source →

Model

Holt-Winters triple exponential smoothing with additive trend and additive seasonal components (statsmodels.tsa.holtwinters.ExponentialSmoothing, seasonal_periods=12). Parameters estimated via maximum likelihood (L-BFGS-B). In-sample AIC: 5,728. Level smoothing α ≈ 0.86.

Uncertainty

Predictive intervals derived from in-sample residual standard deviation ($7.3B) scaled by √h where h is forecast horizon. 80% CI uses z=1.28; 95% CI uses z=1.96. Intervals assume homoscedastic residuals and do not capture structural breaks.
